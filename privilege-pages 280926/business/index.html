Card design guidelines
Aspect 1.75 : 1 — 1120 × 640 px or larger, full bleed. PNG, SVG or PDF. Corners are rounded by the app, so keep anything essential about 6% inside the edges. Keep the bottom-right ~22% × 18% clear — your Privilege № chip overlays there. Your artwork replaces our templates entirely; the logo, colour and font settings above don't apply to it. For now the artwork lives on this device — backend sync will carry it to your customers' cards.
